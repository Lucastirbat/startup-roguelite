1. The Game in One Paragraph

Draw a card each month. A character presents a situation; you pick one of two buttons. Choices move your four stats. Cash at zero or Morale at zero = death, with a post-mortem. Survive and grow Valuation to trigger funding cards — take money (cash up, Equity down) or refuse (risky). Your final score is Equity % × Valuation = your payout. Everything the player touches is: card → two buttons → stat deltas → next card.


2. Stats (4 visible, top bar like the reference game)

StatStartDeathRole💰 Cash$500K$0 = dead−$50K/month base burn😊 Morale700 = deadteam quits🔥 Hype30—decays −5/month; gates funding cards & valuation growth🧢 Equity90%—only decreases; score multiplier

Valuation is derived, not managed: it ratchets up when funding cards are taken and drifts with Hype. Displayed top-left like the reference. Score = Valuation × Equity.

Month counter advances per card (January, February… like the reference). Reaching Month 36 alive = you exit → payout screen (win). Dying earlier still shows your payout at last valuation (usually $0 — startups are illiquid, that's the joke and the lesson).

4. Godot Architecture (deliberately boring)

Scenes (3 total):


Main.tscn — top bar (stats + month), CardView (portrait, text, two buttons), background
GameOver.tscn — post-mortem text + payout + "Start Game" (also serves as win screen with different copy)
Title.tscn — logo, high score, Start (clone the reference layout)


Scripts:


GameState.gd (autoload): stats dict, month, flags array, high score save (user://save.cfg via ConfigFile)
Deck.gd: loads cards.json, draw() = filter by requires → weighted pick → pop if once
Main.gd: apply monthly tick → draw → render → on button press apply effects, check deaths, honor next_card override, advance month


Data: one cards.json in the project. No editor tooling, no Resources pipeline — load with JSON.parse_string(FileAccess.get_file_as_string(...)). Easy for you to author, and easy to generate/expand with an LLM later (or point a Haip-style agent at it for balance runs post-jam).

Art: reference-style 64×64 pixel portraits — 6 characters is enough for 40 cards (Chad, Priya the CTO, Doug the VC, Office Manager, The Angel, Your Mom). Solid-color card frames per character like the red/blue in the screenshots. Font: any pixel font, texture_filter = nearest everywhere.

Juice budget (only if time): stat-change flash on the top bar, button press SFX, month-tick tween. Nothing else.
